Controls

  • Left-drag or WASD: pan · Right-drag or Q / E: rotate · Wheel: zoom
  • Click a square to Stride there (one action per Stride). Blue = 1 action, gold = 2, orange = 3.
  • Pick an action, then a target. Hover a target to see hit chance, modifiers and damage.
  • Esc cancels targeting · Space ends the turn · number keys trigger actions.
  • Click any creature with no action selected to inspect its stat block.

Rules in play

Three actions and a reaction per turn; multiple attack penalty; degrees of success with natural 20/1; flanking and off-guard; lesser, standard and greater cover from terrain and creatures; concealment and hidden flat checks; difficult terrain and elevation; Reactive Strike, Shield Block and champion reactions; dying, wounded, recovery checks and Hero Points; persistent damage; resistances, weaknesses, immunities and regeneration; incapacitation; frightened, sickened, slowed, stunned, prone, grabbed and more.
